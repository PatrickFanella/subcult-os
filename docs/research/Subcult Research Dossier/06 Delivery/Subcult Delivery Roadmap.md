# Subcult Delivery Roadmap

This is a gate-based roadmap, not a promised calendar. Duration depends on existing regression repair, team capacity, provider access and pilot event cadence.

## Phase 0: evidence and scope

Refresh repository/test status, confirm the buyer and territory, choose the first supported ticket provider and establish privacy/consent boundaries. Run recent-event interviews before committing the feature list.

Exit: named pilot candidates, documented workflow baseline, scoped MVP, known deployment blockers and a recovery owner. Stop if the proposed buyer has no budget or no recurring pain.

## Phase 1: joined operational slice

Implement authenticated account linking and one event-to-operations mapping. Let a team create a public occurrence, assign commitments and close the private report.

Exit: cross-workspace access denied, retry behavior correct, public edits reconciled, private fields excluded, browser/mobile workflow usable. Do not require all audience features yet.

## Phase 2: requested communication

Add one verified contact channel, scoped opt-in, a scheduled Signal, pre-send authorization, suppression and clear withdrawal. Introduce provider cost/delivery monitoring.

Exit: complete synthetic delivery/withdrawal tests, protected fallback behavior and a limited real pilot under approved terms. Do not add SMS solely because it looks competitive.

## Phase 3: two-cycle pilot

Run five-to-ten teams through actual events under the supported scope. Keep manual/provider fallbacks and record support labor. Obtain paid continuation offers without bespoke promises.

Exit: repeat workflow use, evidence of willingness to pay, acceptable cost and no unresolved severe safety defect. If value sits in one module, narrow the product.

## Phase 4: independent interoperability

Publish/test the chosen public calendar representation with a second reader. Rebuild the app projection from source data and demonstrate updates/cancellation handling.

Exit: interoperability evidence beyond our own tests, explicit namespace policy, no private information leakage. Upstream acceptance is beneficial but not required for local correctness.

## Phase 5: selective expansion

Consider another integration, richer touring collaboration, native commerce or optional audio only when recurring paid demand and support capacity justify it. Keep Spaces synthetic until its separate gates pass.

## Planning and resourcing

Reserve product/research time as real work. Account for event-night support, documentation, backups, incident handling and provider operations alongside feature development.

The next implementation plan should estimate tasks only after Phase 0. Do not translate the breadth of this dossier directly into an engineering deadline.
