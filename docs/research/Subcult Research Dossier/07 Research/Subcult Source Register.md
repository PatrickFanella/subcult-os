# Subcult Source Register

## Scope and reading rules

Research date: 19 September 2026. This register lists the external URLs actually cited in the dossier, plus the local provenance note. Sources were reviewed during the original proposal research and this expansion; they were not all fetched again at the final packaging moment.

Vendor pages establish what a supplier advertises. They do not independently establish performance, security certification, complete feature absence or our right to integrate. Protocol main-branch pages are moving references, not deployment pins. Obtain exact versions before implementation.

The links below are grouped by evidence type. Each row points to its analytical note, where the claim and limitations are stated. The register is a bibliography, not a copied archive of copyrighted pages.

## Known access and evidence limits

- Laylo's dedicated pricing page did not expose full readable terms; the cited price comes from its own comparison page.
- Eventbrite pricing was observed in the original research; the expansion refresh returned HTTP 429. Reconfirm before a commercial comparison.
- Openstage pricing contains inconsistent higher-tier membership percentages in separate sections; no exact higher-tier percentage is adopted.
- Community schema discussions are proposals/history, not evidence of implementation everywhere.
- No logged-in competitor demo, checkout, contract quote, security audit or API-partner approval was obtained.
- Market context comes from trade-association publications with defined sector/geographic scopes; there is no verified workspace-market denominator.
- Core code/specification references must be pinned and tested against the chosen deployment.

## Repository evidence

[[Subcult Repository Evidence]] records source paths and revisions from both checkouts. It is the authority for statements about local implementation; none of the external sources establishes our application's release readiness.
