## Consequential arithmetic

The pricing model uses:

- 50 × 39 + 20 × 99 = 3,930 MRR; × 12 = 47,160 annualized.
- 250 × 39 + 100 × 99 = 19,650 MRR; × 12 = 235,800 annualized.
- 99 − 8 − 40 × 0.5 = 71 contribution before fixed costs.
- 99 − 8 − 40 × 1 = 51; at two hours, 11.
- 2,000 × 2 × 1 × 0.02 = 80 hypothetical messaging expense before extras.

These calculations are inspectable; none validates the assumed inputs.
