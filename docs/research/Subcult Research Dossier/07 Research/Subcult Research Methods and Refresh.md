# Subcult Research Methods and Refresh

## Work performed

The dossier expands the original business/product proposal into developed Markdown notes with Obsidian wikilinks. Research combined read-only inspection of Subcults/Subcult OS, primary competitor pages, protocol documentation/source and industry publications.

The pack covers 18 named competing or adjacent products across audience, registration, ticketing, venue operations and open events. Coverage depth varies: Laylo receives a dedicated analysis, while narrower peers are grouped by buying job.

The research does not treat marketing language as independent performance evidence. No unsupported competitor feature-absence matrix, national software TAM, traction figure or customer quotation was created.

## Interpretation rules

Use local code for current implementation structure and dated release documents for historical gates. Use official vendor sources for their own product descriptions, not competitors' comparison tables to establish rival weaknesses.

Separate recommendation from observation. A recommended private-data architecture is not an already implemented privacy guarantee. An illustrative price is not willingness-to-pay evidence.

Market sizing is bottom-up scenario analysis because a verified eligible-workspace denominator is missing. KPI design defines future measurements; it does not invent a production baseline.

## Artifact checks

The package is designed for checks of unique filenames, frontmatter, real internal targets, local Markdown paths, graph reachability, empty/placeholder notes and arithmetic. ZIP integrity is checked separately. These checks establish packaging quality, not factual completeness or Obsidian UI behavior.

No community plugins or vault configuration are included. All research lives in ordinary Markdown; no private workspace access is needed to read it. Exact local code paths are provenance text, not required navigation.

The completed structural check found 46 Markdown notes, 239 wikilinks and 54 unique cited external URLs. All notes were reachable from the README, with no missing targets, duplicate stems, orphan notes, nonportable local links or placeholder markers. Twenty scenario arithmetic checks passed. Basic frontmatter structure was checked; this was not a full Obsidian application/UI test. The ZIP was separately checked for archive integrity and byte-for-byte agreement with the source directory.

## Refresh cadence

Before presenting prices externally: check the supplier's current local-currency billing page and obtain quotes where needed. Before implementing AT work: pin specifications/code and review current issues. Before a pilot: refresh source/runtime gates and legal/provider requirements.

After interviews: add de-identified evidence through the template, preserve negative findings and update decisions. After each pilot event: compare actual support/time/cost against assumptions. Review the product position after two cycles.
