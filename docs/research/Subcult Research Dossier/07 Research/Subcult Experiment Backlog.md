# Subcult Experiment Backlog

Experiments are proposed and unrun. Thresholds are decision aids to refine after baseline discovery.

| ID | Hypothesis | Test | Evidence that changes the decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Continuity pain recurs | Recent-event interviews with artifact walkthroughs | Same costly handoff failure across several teams |
| E2 | A real buyer exists | Named scoped offer to budget holders | Paid commitments, not only signups |
| E3 | Operations work without network density | Single-team two-event pilot | Reuse without requiring new audience discovery |
| E4 | Combined flow beats disconnected tools | Paired workflow observation | Less repeated work without added confusion |
| E5 | Scope is understandable | Unmoderated event/crew journey | Correct completion and clear privacy expectations |
| E6 | Communication preserves permission | Synthetic grant/schedule/revoke/send cases | Revocation suppresses delivery; unknown grants refused |
| E7 | Public records interoperate | Second-app calendar reader | Correct time/status/cancellation without bespoke private API |
| E8 | Projection can recover | Crash/backfill/rebuild harness | Correct final state without repeated external actions |
| E9 | Low-price support is viable | Track pilot support and variable cost | Positive contribution under honest labor accounting |
| E10 | Organization access is safe | Remove crew/change owner/revoke app | Private access follows policy without destroying public history |

## Test ordering

Run E1/E2 before broad build. E3/E4 establish product utility. E5/E6/E10 are required safety/usability work before real use. E7/E8 demonstrate the protocol promise. E9 determines whether the offer can scale beyond founder labor.

## Avoid misleading successes

An event created by the founder is not organizer activation. A campaign click is not incremental revenue. A publicly visible record in our own app is not independent interoperability. A test send to the developer is not representative deliverability.

A free pilot can show workflow fit but not paid demand. A one-night event can show functionality but not recurrence. A positive mean can conceal one unacceptable safety failure.

## Reporting

Use [[Subcult Experiment Template]] and record population, exclusions, baseline, dates, exact artifact/revision, result, uncertainty and next decision. Preserve failures and abandoned attempts. Never replace the original pass/fail rule after viewing results without documenting the change.
