# Subcult Luma and Partiful Research

## Luma

Luma combines event registration with calendar/community features. Its public pricing page shows a free plan with a 5% platform fee on paid events and a Plus option advertised at $59/month billed annually with 0% platform fee; payment processing remains a separate consideration. Currency, billing and checkout terms should be reconfirmed for a real customer. [Luma pricing](https://luma.com/pricing).

Its help center documents calendar memberships and event hosts/managers. That makes “persistent community” and multi-person hosting weak standalone novelty claims for Subcult. [Calendar memberships](https://help.luma.com/p/calendar-memberships), [hosts/managers](https://help.luma.com/p/adding-hosts-and-managers-to-your-event).

**Implication:** event creation and RSVP must be quick enough that Subcult's deeper workflow does not feel like mandatory administrative overhead. A production record should be an optional next step from a useful public page.

## Partiful

Partiful is a benchmark for social invitations and guest experience, and it now documents paid ticketing. Its June 2026 fee article says fees vary with event size and ticket price; hosts can absorb or pass them on, and free tickets have no fees. Its $112 guest/$100 host illustration is an example, not evidence of a universal 12% tariff. [Partiful fees](https://help.partiful.com/en-us/articles/15525398-what-fees-does-partiful-charge), [Partiful](https://partiful.com/).

**Implication:** do not dismiss it as “only invitations” or assume paid ticketing is absent. For small social gatherings, simplicity may outweigh Subcult's operational depth.

## What to test

Observe an unfamiliar organizer creating the same event in Subcult and their preferred existing tool. Time to a correct, shareable page matters more than the number of configurable fields. Test a guest without an account on a small screen, including an address change and cancellation.

Subcult should avoid asking attendees to select an identity server before understanding the event. A guest flow can be useful before an account upgrade. Explain public visibility at the moment information is published.

## Unresolved questions

Neither supplier was tested through an authenticated account. Role granularity, export completeness, accessibility behavior, cancellation/refund edge cases and private-location handling need direct verification. Do not describe those capabilities as missing.
