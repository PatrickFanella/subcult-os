# Subcult Music Ticketing Research

## Resident Advisor

RA Pro explicitly serves electronic-music promoters with ticketing, team access and promoter tools. Its existing cultural focus is a direct challenge to any claim that Subcult alone understands scenes. [RA Pro](https://pro.ra.co/promoter).

RA documents ticket fees paid by customers rather than a universal public flat tariff. Its 2026 RA Scan announcement describes offline scanning after data download, among other door features. [Fee explanation](https://support.ra.co/article/177-service-fees), [RA Scan announcement](https://ra.co/news/85673).

**Implication:** discovery context and dependable door operation must be taken seriously. Do not call Subcult's door production-ready merely because a scan route exists.

## DICE

DICE is a relevant music ticketing incumbent. Its published UK standard ticketing terms describe a vendor relationship and conditional implementation/on-site support obligations. Those terms are jurisdiction-specific; they do not establish a universal fee or a freely available Subcult integration. [DICE standard terms](https://support.dice.fm/article/286-standard-ticket-terms).

**Implication:** a host may have contractual obligations that outlive a product preference. Ask about allocations, exclusivity, data access and refunds before proposing replacement or automated imports.

## POSH

POSH's help materials document event creation, refunds/order management and payouts. These are meaningful operator workflows rather than evidence that it is only a promotional page. [Event management](https://support.posh.vip/en/articles/15089963-creating-customizing-and-managing-your-posh-events-the-complete-guide), [refunds and inventory](https://support.posh.vip/en/articles/15090283-how-to-refund-orders-cancel-rsvps-and-manage-ticket-inventory), [payouts](https://support.posh.vip/en/articles/15090213-managing-your-balance-payouts-bank-accounts).

No universal POSH fee or unrestricted integration entitlement is asserted in this dossier.

## Recommended entry strategy

Let the ticket provider remain authoritative for payment, entitlement and refund state. Subcult can initially link to the public purchase page and hold its own private production commitments. If a customer authorizes a roster import, preserve provider IDs, import timestamp, source scope and deletion/correction rules.

An imported attendee is not automatically an announcement subscriber. A ticket QR code is not a general identity credential. Never expose an order number or redeemable credential in a public AT record.

## Verification scenario

Choose one real provider with a willing pilot host. Rehearse purchase, cancellation, reschedule, duplicate delivery, refunded admission and stale roster behavior in its approved test environment. If no safe integration is available, keep provider door operation separate and report aggregate closeout manually.
