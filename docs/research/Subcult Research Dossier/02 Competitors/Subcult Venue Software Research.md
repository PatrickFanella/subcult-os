# Subcult Venue Software Research

## Opendate

Opendate's pricing page distinguishes a free Opener tier, a Marquee tier starting at $4,000/year, and ticketing-linked commercial options. The feature set includes venue booking and operational/financial workflows. Negotiated ticketing terms should not be treated as universally free software. [Opendate pricing](https://www.opendate.io/pricing).

**Implication:** budget planning, offers and settlement already coexist in a specialist product. Subcult cannot credibly claim to invent the operational “source of truth.”

## Prism

Prism describes calendars, deal/payment tracking, financial reporting, settlement and role-specific permissions for venues, promoters and agencies. Its product page also describes opt-in partner box-office research. These are vendor descriptions, not independently tested reliability claims. [Prism product](https://prism.fm/product/).

**Implication:** the professional segment may require substantial depth before considering a switch. A minimal settlement form is not equivalent to real deal tracking or finance workflows.

## Where Subcult might fit

The proposed opportunity is below or beside a professional suite: smaller recurring teams who need explicit commitments and continuity but do not want to adopt every booking/accounting function at once. This is a segment-fit hypothesis, not a proven pricing gap.

An established venue using Prism/Opendate could still use public Subcult discovery or collaborate with a touring Act. Avoid demanding access to private financial data merely to publish an appearance. Integration can be useful without becoming the financial system of record.

## Demo script

Ask an operator to demonstrate a held date becoming a confirmed booking, an artist confirmation, a staffing change, a ticket adjustment, a cancellation and final settlement. Observe how responsibilities and amounts remain traceable.

Then show Subcult's proposed minimal flow and ask which specific recurring task it improves. If the answer is only “the map looks interesting,” do not interpret that as intent to replace operations software.

## Pricing boundary

A higher incumbent headline price does not prove Subcult can profitably undercut it. Onboarding, complex permissions and night-of support may explain a significant portion of the cost. Record the actual support burden and number of billable workspaces before promising low-cost operational breadth.
