# Subcult Audience Platform Research

## Hive

Hive's pricing is tailored to subscriber count, SMS usage and contract length. The page lists unlimited users and email sends across its shown plans, with SMS/MMS usage charges; no universal base subscription quote was established. It displays $0.02/SMS and $0.05/MMS plus carrier fees. [Hive pricing](https://www.hive.co/pricing).

Hive also describes event-marketing automations and revenue reporting connected to ticket activity. These are vendor capabilities, not proof that an attributed purchase was caused by a message. [Automations](https://www.hive.co/features/automations), [reporting](https://www.hive.co/features/reporting).

**Subcult implication:** event-specific CRM and lifecycle campaigns are already a specialist category. “We know what shows your fans attended” is neither novel nor automatically appropriate for all participants.

## Openstage

Openstage advertises Build starting at $130/month, scaling with contactable fan count, and a custom-priced higher tier. Its page includes email, fan profiles, presaves, automations, memberships and ticket unlocks. The same page contains inconsistent higher-tier membership percentages in different sections, so no precise higher-tier transaction fee is adopted here. [Openstage pricing](https://openstage.live/pricing).

Its Fanbase API is pitched as infrastructure for custom logged-in fan experiences. [Fanbase API](https://openstage.live/fanbase-api).

**Subcult implication:** branded community and fan identity are also established competitive territory. Avoid positioning a future API or membership system as uniquely ours. Differentiate on shared public cultural context and independent-team production, if pilots validate that combination.

## Bandsintown

Bandsintown markets free fan contact management tools; its Email Builder help article specifies 10,000 free emails per artist per month. This is a meaningful low-cost alternative for artist communication, not a price for an equivalent venue-operations product. [Fan Manager](https://www.artist.bandsintown.com/fan-manager), [Email Builder](https://help.artists.bandsintown.com/en/articles/7053478-what-is-the-email-builder).

**Subcult implication:** basic email cannot carry the entire paid value proposition. Interview artist teams about what remains difficult after using their existing free tools.

## Cross-category questions

What does the buyer already pay, and which outcome justifies that bill? Which data can be exported with permission evidence? Does an organizer need CRM analytics, operational coordination, or both? Are promised integrations available to small accounts?

Do not replicate engagement scoring merely because a competitor has it. Public or opaque “superfan” ranking can conflict with Subcult's participant-first thesis. If segmentation is useful, begin with declared interests and explicit activity the person understands.
