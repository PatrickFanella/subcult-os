# Competitive Positioning
The original “lush” reference was not definitively identified in this funding pass. Do not quietly substitute Luma or another company as a confirmed match. The prior research dossier retains the broader event-market investigation.

[Laylo](https://laylo.com/) explicitly targets artists, live events, festivals and creators through its drop platform. Treat its direct audience activation focus as a serious competing workflow, not as evidence that it cannot export data or interoperate. No fresh pricing benchmark or feature-by-feature destructive test was performed here.

## Strategic comparison
| Buyer job | Alternative category | Subcult hypothesis | Validation required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Announce a drop and activate fans | Laylo / creator marketing | Connect releases to durable scene and event context | Can an operator achieve comparable activation without extra work? |
| Publish invitations and sell attendance | Event and ticketing platforms | Public records plus recurring operator workflow | Paid pilot, payments/support burden, conversion |
| Run backstage operations | Spreadsheets, task tools, email | Subcult OS commitments, staffing and event handoff | Measured time saved across repeat events |
| Find trustworthy local resources | Directories and community coordinators | Patchwork provenance, corrections and privacy boundaries | Partner verification, stale listing handling, human safety review |
| Build an AT application | SDKs and maintained upstream tools | Reusable lifecycle and authority test infrastructure | External adoption, not only internal reuse |

The moat cannot be exclusive ownership of public AT data. Proposed advantages are domain expertise, operator relationships, coherent user experience, reliable hosted operations and maintainership. Portability lowers switching barriers for customers and competitors alike.

## Market sizing without fabricated TAM
Use a bottom-up scenario: 100 paying organizations × $149/month = $14,900 MRR and $178,800 annualized subscription revenue before churn, taxes, processing and support. This is arithmetic, not a forecast, established addressable market or actual revenue.
Validate the reachable organization count through a named, permission-respecting research list and interviews. Do not equate Bluesky account counts with paying event organizers.

## Discovery plan
Interview ten operators about the last event they ran: record systems used, repeated handoffs, missed commitments, messaging permissions, costs and renewal decisions. Ask for a paid bounded pilot only after observing a repeated costly problem. Stop or change the wedge if portability is admired but nobody pays for workflow improvement.
