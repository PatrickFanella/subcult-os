## Method and coverage gaps
Repository inspection covered the specified Patchwork and DSA-suite roots and bounded adjacent inventory; Subcults/OS uses the earlier source review in this task. Funding research prioritized official program pages and company-authored financing announcements.
Missing: undisclosed investors, current investor mandates/contact qualification, direct funder confirmation, complete nationwide/local US philanthropy, final legal eligibility, current application portal acceptance and future deadlines not yet announced. No general “AT Protocol Foundation” grant program is invented.
Some program pages omit award size or open intake; these remain unknown. No private professional database or confidential deal flow was accessed.
