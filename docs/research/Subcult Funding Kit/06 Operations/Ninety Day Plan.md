# Ninety Day Plan
Proposed sequence; no meetings or deadlines have been scheduled externally.

## Days 1–14
Founder resolves [[Data Room and Submission Gates]]. Recheck rights and current repository revisions. Choose exactly one commons package and one culture pilot. Create a cash forecast with actual numbers. Qualify NLnet's US exception and fiscal-host fit. Prepare a disposable demonstration with no real membership or beneficiary data.

## Days 15–30
Conduct ten operator/problem interviews as a target; record permission and findings, not invented quotes. Seek two external technical reviewers and one paid culture discovery engagement. Publish nothing without approval. Prepare a scoped grant answer bank in the founder's voice; retain AI provenance where required.

## Days 31–60
Deliver lifecycle fixtures and one complete pilot workflow rehearsal. Review failed cases, rights, privacy and accessibility. Founder approves any application or email before sending. Recheck November call deadlines and timezone. Negotiate milestone payment timing and avoid taking obligations unsupported by cash.

## Days 61–90
Obtain an external reproduction attempt and a customer continuation decision as targets. Produce a public-safe evidence report and maintenance plan. Expand only if funding, partner authorization and verification justify it.

## Weekly operating rhythm
Two days Commons; one and a half culture; half day Patchwork qualification; half day fundraising/admin; half day buffer and review. This is a practical approximation, reconciled to actual time records rather than a second billing allocation.
Maintain one delivery board with owner, cost code, acceptance evidence, due date and stop condition. Pause new project starts when any funded milestone slips.

## Month-three decision
Continue if the work has a credible payer or grant path, reproducible technical progress and manageable operational obligations. Otherwise narrow to paid integration plus one maintained commons component. Do not interpret polite investor conversations as financing.
