# Data Room and Submission Gates
This kit is preparation material, not an authorized application. Keep identity, banking, taxpayer information, cap tables and sensitive contracts out of the public Obsidian copy.

## Founder / entity
- [ ] Legal name, concise factual biography, relevant employment and project history approved.
- [ ] Entity name, state, tax classification, ownership/cap table and signing authority verified.
- [ ] Monthly founder cash requirement, health/tax costs, existing cash and obligations entered.
- [ ] Equity appetite and mission/nonprofit preference decided.
- [ ] Time availability and other commitments disclosed.

## Rights / evidence
- [ ] Current revision and dirty-state record for each included repository.
- [ ] License/contributor/dependency audit; missing forum/seats licenses resolved or projects excluded.
- [ ] Data and image redistribution permissions reviewed; private workbooks excluded.
- [ ] Demo uses synthetic or explicitly approved records.
- [ ] Current passing checks and deployment evidence supplied only for claims that need them.
- [ ] Partner/customer letters real, dated and permissioned.
- [ ] Revenue and adoption supported by records; no repository counts presented as traction.

## Agreement
- [ ] Exact program intake, US eligibility, permitted costs and AI policy rechecked.
- [ ] Budget currency, indirect costs, sponsor fees and disbursement schedule reviewed.
- [ ] Deliverables, IP, reporting, termination and maintenance obligations understood.
- [ ] Other applications/awards disclosed and duplicate charges prevented.
- [ ] Political/charitable separation reviewed by qualified US counsel.
- [ ] Founder approves submission, attachments and external representations.

## Shareable data room structure
01 Narrative and deck; 02 rights-cleared technical evidence; 03 budget and milestones; 04 public governance and maintenance; 05 verified partner letters.
Private restricted folder outside this kit: identity/tax/bank documents, cap table, contracts, sensitive threat model details and personnel records.
