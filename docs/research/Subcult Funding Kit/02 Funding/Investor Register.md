## Qualification before contact
For every prospect record: firm or individual, official public contact route, current role, geographic/entity/stage mandate, potential competing investments, relevant partner, warm introduction source if actually known, preferred instrument and minimum ownership. Leave these blank until verified; do not guess emails.

Prioritize Skyseed for explicit AT fit. Then selectively qualify Betaworks, Bloomberg Beta, True Ventures, Neo and Tangled's disclosed institutional backers for the specific company stage and thesis. This ordering is an analyst judgment, not a probability ranking or evidence of current interest.
Large-company round participation does not establish appetite for a solo founder's pre-seed house. Lead with culture product distribution and recurring buyer value, not twelve unrelated applications.

The Graze release references angels affiliated with Mozilla and Protocol Labs; that alone is not an investment by those organizations. Knight has both an investment precedent and philanthropic activity; keep the routes separate. Kris Nóva appears in the historical seed announcement and must not enter an outreach pipeline.
