# Funding Strategy
## Recommendation
Buy focused founder time through a mix of scoped open-source work, paid implementation and, if desired, a small aligned investment. Do not depend on a single speculative foundation award. The house is a coherent operating thesis; grants should usually fund one reusable work package, and investors should see one commercial wedge.

## First six conversations
1. Skyseed: qualify appetite for culture infrastructure plus ecosystem contribution. Use [[House Investor Deck]].
2. AT Protocol Community Fund: discuss existing location/organization work and a commons collaboration. Use [[House Public Goods Deck]].
3. NLnet: ask whether your actual US profile and European use case qualify before spending a week on a full application.
4. FUTO: request a small bounded component award, not a salary rescue.
5. A prospective cultural operator: sell a short paid discovery/migration pilot with clear deliverables.
6. A potential fiscal sponsor: clarify project fit and money/IP/control boundaries before naming the sponsor in applications.

These are recommended draft routes, not messages sent or commitments secured. [[Funding Register]] contains source links and current status.

## Timing
September 20–30: finalize founder/entity facts; verify rights; collect one reproducible technical demonstration; qualify partners and funders. Attend/consult NLnet's published September 30 office-hour route if available and useful.
October 1–15: recheck Raft intake notice; write one scoped grant in the founder's own voice; seek two technical review letters without inventing endorsement.
October 16–November 3: independent review, budget/AI provenance reconciliation, submission only after founder authorization. Restack cutoff is noon CET, not US local midnight.
November–December: paid pilot and maintainer-sponsorship work; do not stop product work waiting for grant decisions. Interledger event support is only worth pursuing if an authentic open-payments event is already part of the mission.

## Funding fit is not a ranked probability
High strategic fit: AT ecosystem relationships.
Actionable conditional grant: Restack, FUTO.
Mature-infrastructure route: Sovereign Tech Fund/Fellowship only after showing downstream criticality.
Specialized route: OTF for genuine internet-freedom threats, Interledger for genuine payment interoperability.
Longer-horizon philanthropy: public-benefit partners and nonprofit model first.
Closed/watch: old Bluesky grants, MOSS, this Fast Forward deadline, historical accelerator cycles.

## Resilience
Maintain a zero-award forecast. Start with cash already in the bank, subtract actual monthly obligations, then include grants only after signed terms and reliable payment schedules. A grant reimbursing milestones may still require bridge cash. Cloud credits do not pay rent. Event grants do not justify reallocating event budgets into unrestricted house development.
At 90 days without qualified funding or paid demand, narrow to a single component and service offering. Do not increase fixed payroll on the strength of pipeline names.
