# The user and problem

Community facilitators and governance-software builders

Meeting procedure and public outcomes need traceability without disclosing deliberation or ballots.

---

# What we propose

Develop the event-sourced simulator and reusable approved aggregate-publication contracts.

A bounded work package, not an all-at-once platform launch.

---

# What exists today

Implemented meeting simulator; experimental AT adapter; explicitly no production PDS integration.

Source evidence is not current production verification.

---

# The interoperability boundary

Public approved aggregate outcomes; private attendance, rosters, deliberation, credentials and ballots.

Public records should be reusable; private operations stay deliberately separate.

---

# Delivery and proof

Replay deterministic fixtures; prove private fields cannot enter published artifacts; accessible facilitator rehearsal.

Publish limitations and reproducible fixtures; retain failed evidence.

---

# Sustainability

Training, support and grant-backed maintenance; rights reviewed for all instructional content.

Complement facilitation tools with reproducibility and explicit publication control.

---

# The ask and decision

Proposed $25,000 four-month public/private governance R&D package, not an election system.

No secret ballots, binding voting, legal procedure certification or copied Robert's Rules text.

Next: qualify scope, rights and counterpart before an agreement.
