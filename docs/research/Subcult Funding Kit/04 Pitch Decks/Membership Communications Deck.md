# Membership Communications

An AT-focused Subcult.tv initiative

Document and test entitlement/revocation across an AT-adjacent identity service and Matrix/OIDC.

---

# The user and problem

Organizations needing governed communication access

Membership revocation and identity handoff can fail across independently operated communication systems.

---

# What we propose

Document and test entitlement/revocation across an AT-adjacent identity service and Matrix/OIDC.

A bounded work package, not an all-at-once platform launch.

---

# What exists today

Local-contract architecture; direct AT publication not evidenced and live authorized account gates remain open.

Source evidence is not current production verification.

---

# The interoperability boundary

Private membership and communication metadata; no public membership graph.

Public records should be reusable; private operations stay deliberately separate.

---

# Delivery and proof

Two authorized test accounts in isolated topology; revocation and recovery evidence; independent review before security claims.

Publish limitations and reproducible fixtures; retain failed evidence.

---

# Sustainability

Infrastructure support contracts only after operational qualification.

Use Matrix ecosystem components rather than claiming to replace them; no affiliation with Signal.

---

# The ask and decision

Proposed $20,000 scoped interoperability and independent security review, not a new messenger launch.

E2EE does not hide service metadata or defeat a compromised browser origin; no secure-production claim.
