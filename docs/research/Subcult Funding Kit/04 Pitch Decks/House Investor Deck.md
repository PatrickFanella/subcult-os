# Subcult.tv

Portable public culture. Private tools for the people organizing it.

Proposed US-based AT-focused software house.

---

# The first buyer

Independent venues, promoters and artist collectives running recurring events.

Their public presence and backstage work are fragmented.

---

# The wedge

One coherent artist / scene / venue / event presence, connected to private commitments, staffing and organizer handoffs.

Begin with a bounded paid pilot.

---

# Why AT

Public identity and records can outlive a single application.

Subcult adds the domain workflow, useful discovery and reliable operations—not just a login button.

---

# What exists

Subcults and OS have source foundations for cultural records and organizational workflows.

Patchwork and civic prototypes inform reusable boundaries. They are not all launched businesses.

---

# Business model hypothesis

Free public discovery; paid organizational workspaces, hosting and support.

Test $49 / $149 / $399 monthly tiers. No current prices or revenue are asserted.

---

# Market entry

One regional scene, recurring operators, hands-on migration and paid design partners.

Target repeat workflow value before broad consumer acquisition.

---

# Competition

Laylo competes for audience activation; ticketing and task tools cover adjacent jobs.

Win through coherent operator workflow. Public data is not an exclusive moat.

---

# House discipline

One culture wedge plus one Commons package.

Maintain open infrastructure; separate restricted public-benefit funds and commercial costs.

---

# Eighteen-month plan

Qualify the workflow, obtain paid continuation decisions, prove lifecycle/recovery, and document external reuse.

All partner/adoption milestones are targets.
