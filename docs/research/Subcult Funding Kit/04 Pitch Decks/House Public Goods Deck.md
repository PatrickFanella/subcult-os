# Subcult Commons

Make AT applications dependable enough for real community coordination.

A proposed public-benefit program within Subcult.tv.

---

# The shared problem

Small teams repeatedly implement publication authority, ingestion, correction, deletion and recovery.

Unclear boundaries can expose private data or silently diverge.

---

# The proposal

A reusable lifecycle and safe-publication toolkit.

Synthetic fixtures, reference readers, migration guidance and independent reproduction.

---

# Why these reference applications

Culture: recurring event records.
Patchwork: resources, provenance and location boundaries.
Organization prototypes: authority and approved artifacts.

---

# Evidence, honestly

Real source implementations and documented constraints exist.

Patchwork remains pre-alpha; civic authority schemas are provisional. Current production qualification is not asserted.

---

# What everyone receives

Rights-cleared source and fixtures, documented failures, maintenance process and external-reader examples.

Useful without adopting Subcult's commercial service.

---

# What remains private

Membership, contacts, consent, exact personal location, moderation evidence, ballots and operational credentials.

Public portability does not make sensitive data safe to publish.

---

# Six-month acceptance

Two independent reader/reproduction targets.
Negative authorization and sensitive-field tests.
Recovery and namespace migration evidence.

Targets—not adoption claims.

---

# Stewardship

Collaborate with existing AT maintainers before new schemas.

Compatible licensing, review process, issue triage and a second maintainer pathway.

---

# The scoped ask

Illustrative $50,000 package:
$30k implementation; $8k review; $5k docs/fixtures; $4k infrastructure; $3k administration.

Capacity and allowable costs confirmed before award.

---

# Integrity of funding

No double-billed hours. No partisan campaign activity. No implied sponsor or DSA endorsement.

Open outputs and transparent limitations.

---

# Next step

Qualify program, US eligibility and actual beneficiary fit.

Agree one package, acceptance criteria, payment timing and maintenance obligations.
