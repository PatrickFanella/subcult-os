# Subcult Culture Speaker Notes
Companion to [[Subcult Culture Deck]]. Target length: 5–7 minutes. State that this is a proposal and distinguish prototypes from qualified services.

## Slide 1: Subcult Culture
Open with the beneficiary, not the protocol acronym. Explain why this work belongs in a focused house.

Anchor: An AT-focused Subcult.tv initiative Combine artist, scene, venue, event and touring records with private commitments, staffing and event handoffs.

## Slide 2: The user and problem
Ask the listener whether this problem matches their actual experience. Do not invent interview quotes.

Anchor: Recurring independent venues and artist collectives Public scene discovery and backstage operations are fragmented across different tools.

## Slide 3: What we propose
Describe one concrete workflow from beginning to end. Explain the scope deliberately excluded.

Anchor: Combine artist, scene, venue, event and touring records with private commitments, staffing and event handoffs. A bounded work package, not an all-at-once platform launch.

## Slide 4: What exists today
Offer the exact repository anchors from the proposal. No tests or live deployment were run for this funding review.

Anchor: Implemented source foundations; integrated paid workflow and production qualification remain unverified. Source evidence is not current production verification.

## Slide 5: The interoperability boundary
Walk through one allowed public field and one prohibited private field. Portability is not privacy or consent.

Anchor: Public artist/venue/event records; private contact consent, staffing and financial operations. Public records should be reusable; private operations stay deliberately separate.

## Slide 6: Delivery and proof
Explain who independently verifies acceptance and what happens if the test fails. Counts are proposed targets, not achieved metrics.

Anchor: Three operator discovery sessions; one end-to-end event rehearsal; a paid continuation decision. Publish limitations and reproducible fixtures; retain failed evidence.

## Slide 7: Sustainability
Explain who would pay and why. All pricing or partnership assumptions still require validation.

Anchor: Hosted organization subscriptions plus migration/support; pricing is a test hypothesis. Laylo and event platforms own important audience workflows; win by reducing repeated operator work, not claiming feature parity.

## Slide 8: The ask and decision
Make the bounded ask, then pause. Do not imply a specific funder has agreed or that funding automatically makes the product safe.

Anchor: A founder-led twelve-week paid design pilot, funded inside the house's culture allocation; suggested $15,000 scoped delivery envelope. Do not promise refunds, offline check-in, audited accounting or fan conversion without evidence. Next: qualify scope, rights and counterpart before an agreement.

## Questions to prepare
Why not contribute to an existing project? What rights do you hold? Who will maintain this after the award? What evidence will an outsider reproduce? How much founder time is actually available? What fails closed if funding ends?
