## Backup answers
Why a house? One shared technical thesis and stewardship practice, not simultaneous launches.
Why fund this now? A scoped package can be independently tested; demand and broader adoption still require proof.
Who owns the work? Pending contributor/license and entity diligence; no exclusive rights promised.
What if an upstream rejects the design? Publish the evidence, adapt to feedback and avoid claiming standard adoption.
