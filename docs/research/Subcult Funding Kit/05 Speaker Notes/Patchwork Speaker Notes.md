# Patchwork Speaker Notes
Companion to [[Patchwork Deck]]. Target length: 5–7 minutes. State that this is a proposal and distinguish prototypes from qualified services.

## Slide 1: Patchwork
Open with the beneficiary, not the protocol acronym. Explain why this work belongs in a focused house.

Anchor: An AT-focused Subcult.tv initiative Qualify public AT resource records, private stewardship and coarse-location discovery; extract reusable safety and lifecycle fixtures.

## Slide 2: The user and problem
Ask the listener whether this problem matches their actual experience. Do not invent interview quotes.

Anchor: Public-resource stewards and mutual-aid coordinators Resource information goes stale while public discovery can expose people seeking help.

## Slide 3: What we propose
Describe one concrete workflow from beginning to end. Explain the scope deliberately excluded.

Anchor: Qualify public AT resource records, private stewardship and coarse-location discovery; extract reusable safety and lifecycle fixtures. A bounded work package, not an all-at-once platform launch.

## Slide 4: What exists today
Offer the exact repository anchors from the proposal. No tests or live deployment were run for this funding review.

Anchor: Canonical checkout 29bb643e8eab is explicitly pre-alpha / NO-GO; source review is not live-service verification. Source evidence is not current production verification.

## Slide 5: The interoperability boundary
Walk through one allowed public field and one prohibited private field. Portability is not privacy or consent.

Anchor: Public reviewed resource records and coarse requests; private exact personal locations, evidence, membership and coordination. Public records should be reusable; private operations stay deliberately separate.

## Slide 6: Delivery and proof
Explain who independently verifies acceptance and what happens if the test fails. Counts are proposed targets, not achieved metrics.

Anchor: Independent privacy/accessibility review; disposable PDS lifecycle test; partner-verification gate before any protected pilot. Publish limitations and reproducible fixtures; retain failed evidence.

## Slide 7: Sustainability
Explain who would pay and why. All pricing or partnership assumptions still require validation.

Anchor: Maintenance sponsorship and mission-aligned hosted support; no monetization of beneficiary data. Directories compete on coverage; this proposal competes on transparent provenance and safe coordination, pending validation.

## Slide 8: The ask and decision
Make the bounded ask, then pause. Do not imply a specific funder has agreed or that funding automatically makes the product safe.

Anchor: Proposed $40,000 protected-pilot work package over six months, only with dedicated capacity and an eligible sponsor; alternative to overlapping commons work. No current impact, nationwide completeness, verified availability, native mobile or production-readiness claim. Next: qualify scope, rights and counterpart before an agreement.

## Questions to prepare
Why not contribute to an existing project? What rights do you hold? Who will maintain this after the award? What evidence will an outsider reproduce? How much founder time is actually available? What fails closed if funding ends?
