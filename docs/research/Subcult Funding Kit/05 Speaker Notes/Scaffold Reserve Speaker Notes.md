# Scaffold Reserve Speaker Notes
Companion to [[Scaffold Reserve Deck]]. Target length: 5–7 minutes. State that this is a proposal and distinguish prototypes from qualified services.

## Slide 1: Scaffold Reserve
Open with the beneficiary, not the protocol acronym. Explain why this work belongs in a focused house.

Anchor: An AT-focused Subcult.tv initiative Retain the minimal Go/React scaffold without inventing a new product.

## Slide 2: The user and problem
Ask the listener whether this problem matches their actual experience. Do not invent interview quotes.

Anchor: Internal maintainers only Too many prototypes can create maintenance obligations before a validated product exists.

## Slide 3: What we propose
Describe one concrete workflow from beginning to end. Explain the scope deliberately excluded.

Anchor: Retain the minimal Go/React scaffold without inventing a new product. A bounded work package, not an all-at-once platform launch.

## Slide 4: What exists today
Offer the exact repository anchors from the proposal. No tests or live deployment were run for this funding review.

Anchor: Scaffold only at 06d9fc459b5f; no domain-specific AT implementation. Source evidence is not current production verification.

## Slide 5: The interoperability boundary
Walk through one allowed public field and one prohibited private field. Portability is not privacy or consent.

Anchor: No new public records or user dataset planned. Public records should be reusable; private operations stay deliberately separate.

## Slide 6: Delivery and proof
Explain who independently verifies acceptance and what happens if the test fails. Counts are proposed targets, not achieved metrics.

Anchor: Document scope; keep baseline runnable; do not launch without a distinct validated problem. Publish limitations and reproducible fixtures; retain failed evidence.

## Slide 7: Sustainability
Explain who would pay and why. All pricing or partnership assumptions still require validation.

Anchor: No standalone business model proposed. Mature templates exist; maintaining another is not itself a venture thesis.

## Slide 8: The ask and decision
Make the bounded ask, then pause. Do not imply a specific funder has agreed or that funding automatically makes the product safe.

Anchor: $0 external product ask; routine maintenance inside existing administration capacity. Not counted as a launched product, interoperability evidence or customer traction. Next: qualify scope, rights and counterpart before an agreement.

## Questions to prepare
Why not contribute to an existing project? What rights do you hold? Who will maintain this after the award? What evidence will an outsider reproduce? How much founder time is actually available? What fails closed if funding ends?
