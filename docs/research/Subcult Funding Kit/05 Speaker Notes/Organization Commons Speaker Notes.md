# Organization Commons Speaker Notes
Companion to [[Organization Commons Deck]]. Target length: 5–7 minutes. State that this is a proposal and distinguish prototypes from qualified services.

## Slide 1: Organization Commons
Open with the beneficiary, not the protocol acronym. Explain why this work belongs in a focused house.

Anchor: An AT-focused Subcult.tv initiative Generalize provisional organizational records, authority policy and recovery fixtures without publishing member data.

## Slide 2: The user and problem
Ask the listener whether this problem matches their actual experience. Do not invent interview quotes.

Anchor: Independent organizations and AT application maintainers A signed record proves authorship, not that someone is authorized to speak for an organization.

## Slide 3: What we propose
Describe one concrete workflow from beginning to end. Explain the scope deliberately excluded.

Anchor: Generalize provisional organizational records, authority policy and recovery fixtures without publishing member data. A bounded work package, not an all-at-once platform launch.

## Slide 4: What exists today
Offer the exact repository anchors from the proposal. No tests or live deployment were run for this funding review.

Anchor: Implemented provisional AT prototype; namespace us.dsaslate.temp is not stable or officially adopted. Source evidence is not current production verification.

## Slide 5: The interoperability boundary
Walk through one allowed public field and one prohibited private field. Portability is not privacy or consent.

Anchor: Public approved organization artifacts; private authority review, sessions and membership. Public records should be reusable; private operations stay deliberately separate.

## Slide 6: Delivery and proof
Explain who independently verifies acceptance and what happens if the test fails. Counts are proposed targets, not achieved metrics.

Anchor: Revoked-writer negative tests; independent reader; namespace migration fixtures and public threat-boundary documentation. Publish limitations and reproducible fixtures; retain failed evidence.

## Slide 7: Sustainability
Explain who would pay and why. All pricing or partnership assumptions still require validation.

Anchor: Open maintenance sponsorship, integration support and downstream cofunding. Build on existing identity/organization work; do not establish a Subcult-controlled universal authority registry.

## Slide 8: The ask and decision
Make the bounded ask, then pause. Do not imply a specific funder has agreed or that funding automatically makes the product safe.

Anchor: Proposed $30,000 four-month authority/compatibility package; schedule within Commons, not as another full-time project. No official DSA authority, stable namespace, adopted standard or production PDS proof. Next: qualify scope, rights and counterpart before an agreement.

## Questions to prepare
Why not contribute to an existing project? What rights do you hold? Who will maintain this after the award? What evidence will an outsider reproduce? How much founder time is actually available? What fails closed if funding ends?
