# Community Forum Speaker Notes
Companion to [[Community Forum Deck]]. Target length: 5–7 minutes. State that this is a proposal and distinguish prototypes from qualified services.

## Slide 1: Community Forum
Open with the beneficiary, not the protocol acronym. Explain why this work belongs in a focused house.

Anchor: An AT-focused Subcult.tv initiative Prepare a licensable generic moderation and content-lifecycle reference implementation.

## Slide 2: The user and problem
Ask the listener whether this problem matches their actual experience. Do not invent interview quotes.

Anchor: Small governed communities Moderation, reversibility and visibility rules are difficult to make consistent.

## Slide 3: What we propose
Describe one concrete workflow from beginning to end. Explain the scope deliberately excluded.

Anchor: Prepare a licensable generic moderation and content-lifecycle reference implementation. A bounded work package, not an all-at-once platform launch.

## Slide 4: What exists today
Offer the exact repository anchors from the proposal. No tests or live deployment were run for this funding review.

Anchor: Implemented local forum; no direct AT implementation; missing project-wide license and unsupported production deployment. Source evidence is not current production verification.

## Slide 5: The interoperability boundary
Walk through one allowed public field and one prohibited private field. Portability is not privacy or consent.

Anchor: Public approved topics only; private reports, notifications, follows, moderator actions and retained data. Public records should be reusable; private operations stay deliberately separate.

## Slide 6: Delivery and proof
Explain who independently verifies acceptance and what happens if the test fails. Counts are proposed targets, not achieved metrics.

Anchor: First rights/license decision; then visibility matrix, reversible moderation fixtures and integration boundary tests. Publish limitations and reproducible fixtures; retain failed evidence.

## Slide 7: Sustainability
Explain who would pay and why. All pricing or partnership assumptions still require validation.

Anchor: Future hosted support, subject to license and actual customer demand. Competes with mature forums; fund reusable moderation contracts, not another undifferentiated forum.

## Slide 8: The ask and decision
Make the bounded ask, then pause. Do not imply a specific funder has agreed or that funding automatically makes the product safe.

Anchor: Deferred $15,000 lifecycle extraction hypothesis after licensing and rights approval; no current OSS grant submission. Do not call it open source until licensed; DID handoff alone is not AT integration. Next: qualify scope, rights and counterpart before an agreement.

## Questions to prepare
Why not contribute to an existing project? What rights do you hold? Who will maintain this after the award? What evidence will an outsider reproduce? How much founder time is actually available? What fails closed if funding ends?
