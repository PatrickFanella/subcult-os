# Civic Evidence Lab Speaker Notes
Companion to [[Civic Evidence Lab Deck]]. Target length: 5–7 minutes. State that this is a proposal and distinguish prototypes from qualified services.

## Slide 1: Civic Evidence Lab
Open with the beneficiary, not the protocol acronym. Explain why this work belongs in a focused house.

Anchor: An AT-focused Subcult.tv initiative Extract source-lock, release-receipt and provenance patterns; explore public AT receipts only as future R&D.

## Slide 2: The user and problem
Ask the listener whether this problem matches their actual experience. Do not invent interview quotes.

Anchor: Nonpartisan civic-data researchers and public-interest publishers Public records need traceable sources, explicit missingness and correction histories.

## Slide 3: What we propose
Describe one concrete workflow from beginning to end. Explain the scope deliberately excluded.

Anchor: Extract source-lock, release-receipt and provenance patterns; explore public AT receipts only as future R&D. A bounded work package, not an all-at-once platform launch.

## Slide 4: What exists today
Offer the exact repository anchors from the proposal. No tests or live deployment were run for this funding review.

Anchor: Implemented civic research foundation; no AT integration; no project-wide license found. Broader release claims not independently verified here. Source evidence is not current production verification.

## Slide 5: The interoperability boundary
Walk through one allowed public field and one prohibited private field. Portability is not privacy or consent.

Anchor: Public licensed source receipts; transient submitted addresses excluded from logs/storage and public output. Public records should be reusable; private operations stay deliberately separate.

## Slide 6: Delivery and proof
Explain who independently verifies acceptance and what happens if the test fails. Counts are proposed targets, not achieved metrics.

Anchor: Reproduce a rights-cleared dataset release; trace each correction; demonstrate no address retention in controlled tests. Publish limitations and reproducible fixtures; retain failed evidence.

## Slide 7: Sustainability
Explain who would pay and why. All pricing or partnership assumptions still require validation.

Anchor: Research partnerships and open-data maintenance; no voter profiling or campaign services in this proposal. Compete on provenance and uncertainty rather than electoral persuasion or prediction.

## Slide 8: The ask and decision
Make the bounded ask, then pause. Do not imply a specific funder has agreed or that funding automatically makes the product safe.

Anchor: Deferred $25,000 nonpartisan provenance package after rights, data-reuse and legal review; no campaign activity. No current AT product, full national enriched coverage, private workbook reuse or DSA endorsement. Next: qualify scope, rights and counterpart before an agreement.

## Questions to prepare
Why not contribute to an existing project? What rights do you hold? Who will maintain this after the award? What evidence will an outsider reproduce? How much founder time is actually available? What fails closed if funding ends?
