## Presentation use
Deck files contain Markdown slides separated by a line containing three hyphens. Speaker notes are separate so presenter instructions do not appear on slides. Open in Obsidian's Slides view if enabled, or adapt the text in a slide editor.
No PPTX/PDF was generated or visually rendered. This is a complete editable narrative deck set, not a claim of finished native slide artifacts. Review slide fit in your chosen presentation environment before sending.
