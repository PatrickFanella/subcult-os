# Subcult.tv — an AT Protocol house
Research snapshot: 2026-09-19, America/Chicago. Working proposal kit; not submitted, funded, legally approved, or production certification.

Start with [[House Proposal]], [[Funding Strategy]], [[Runway Model]], and [[Funding Register]]. Present [[House Investor Deck]] or [[House Public Goods Deck]], not both narratives at once.

## How to use
Drop this whole directory into an Obsidian vault. All internal links use unique note names. Decks are editable Markdown with horizontal slide separators and separate speaker notes. No plugins are required for reading. Enable Obsidian's built-in Slides core plugin to present a deck. No finished PowerPoint or PDF is included: the required PowerPoint authoring runtime was unavailable.

The earlier **Subcult Research Dossier** is preserved separately. This kit stands alone and extends that market research into a funding strategy. Local repository paths in evidence notes are audit anchors, not portable source bundles.

## Decisions still required
Founder legal name and biography; entity/state/tax status; monthly cash need and health/tax costs; current unrestricted cash; full-time availability; ownership and contributor permissions; actual customers and commitments; willingness to accept equity; public-benefit governance preference. Numbers in this kit are explicit planning scenarios, not your financial facts.

“All funders” means a broad public-source landscape, including disclosed AT-company investors, relevant open-source programs, fiscal hosts and adjacent foundations. It cannot include undisclosed investors or guarantee every opportunity. Historical backers are not evidence of open applications. Recheck deadlines and eligibility immediately before outreach.
